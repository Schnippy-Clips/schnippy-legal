Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Schnippy Clips is a private, personal software tool operated solely by its owner for uploading video clips to the owner's own accounts on platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. It is not a public service, and it does not collect data from, or act on behalf of, any other user.

What Data We Access

To publish content to the owner's own accounts, Schnippy Clips uses official platform APIs (such as the YouTube Data API) and accesses only:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to authenticate the owner's own accounts
  • Basic channel/account information (e.g. channel name, channel ID) needed to confirm the correct account and upload target
  • Upload status and metadata for videos the owner publishes through the tool

How Data Is Stored

All data, including OAuth tokens and channel information, is stored only locally on the owner's own machine. No data is transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any external server operated by a third party, other than the platform APIs themselves (e.g. Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram) that are directly required to publish content.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose any data to third parties. Data is used exclusively for the operation of this private tool by its owner.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained locally for as long as the tool is in use. The owner may delete all locally stored data, including OAuth tokens, at any time by removing the tool's local data directory. Revoking platform API access (e.g. via the Google Account permissions page) immediately invalidates any associated OAuth tokens.
